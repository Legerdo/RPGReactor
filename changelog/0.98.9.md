# RPG Reactor 0.98.9 (in development)

0.98.9 builds rocks and cliffs like bricks, makes battles and 3D maps faster, and fixes a long list of things found by using the editor and the game in unusual orders. The full technical list is in the [editor changelog](../editor/changelog/0.98.9.md).

### Added

**Rocks and cliffs**

- **A Rock slot in the Build bar.** Pick a size (Boulder, Rock, Cliff, Spire, Ledge, Slab), click to lay a rock, click one to stack another on it. Rocks laid side by side and stacked melt into one craggy mass with ledges and cracks, so a row of bricks reads as a cliff.
- **Drag lays a row, Ctrl-drag fills an area.** With Vary on, each rock gets its own size, a slight turn and roughness; the ghost always shows the rock the next click lays.
- **Tops and tints.** A rock's top can wear any material or the map's own ground, so a cliff top is the grass around it. Tint colors its sides. The Demo gains Rock, Grass, Moss and Snow materials.
- **Walked on as drawn.** A party stands on the rock it sees, is stopped by its bulges and walks under a raised rock.

### Improved

- **Faster battles.** A battle's 3D room draws straight into the game's own picture instead of being copied through the CPU every frame, and once per frame shown. A Demo battle on a software renderer went from 249 to about 95 ms a frame.
- **Faster 3D maps and editor.** Lights are sent to the GPU only when they change, the reflection capture rests while nothing shiny is on screen, reflections and glass no longer make every material reload its shader, and off-screen videos stop uploading frames.
- **Ponds follow the ground.** Digging under a lake or stamping a building into it refits the water, so no see-through patches are left behind.

### Fixed

- **Undo does what you expect in every tool.** Ctrl+Z mid-drag, after switching tools, in Lighting, Media Surfaces and Event mode, and across terrain and building steps takes back the right thing and keeps other tools' work.
- **Nothing is lost by accident.** Copy and Paste with a building slot in hand no longer pastes a copy of the whole map; clearing a structure's name no longer deletes its file; Import Project refuses the project you have open; the event editor is safe from undo underneath it.
- **Escape closes the innermost dialog**, not the whole Database. Viewing a record no longer counts as an edit.
- **Events of any size own their whole footprint** in the 2D editor too.
- **In the game:** a Text Input no longer leaves a key held, the menu stays shut while a fatal fall ends the game, and memory no longer grows with every battle.
- **Faster on weak graphics.** On an Intel UHD 630 the start map now walks at about 115 FPS at 1080p, where it began at 17: the 3D is drawn at three quarters of the screen and enlarged sharp, lighting and light haze are worked out at a smaller size and filtered up, lights that hold still are baked, moving lights are gathered once a frame, shadows draw simplified outlines, and the editor draws only when something changes. New things appear as they load instead of stopping the game.
- **Clean edges on fast settings.** Models no longer pick up blocks of the ground's light around their edges, laser beams stay crisp lines, and some floors take the shadows they had been missing.
- **Ryzen laptops on Linux** get the settings made for integrated graphics.
- **Every party member casts a shadow** on integrated graphics, not only the leader.
- **A GPU reset no longer breaks the game.** After a driver reset, sleep or graphics crash the 3D map, battle rooms, window text and effects come back instead of a black screen or empty windows.
- **Importer:** titles in Japanese and Western European games read correctly, maps numbered 1000 and up import, and XP imports no longer risk running the editor out of memory.
