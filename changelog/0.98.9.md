# RPG Reactor 0.98.9 (in development)

0.98.9 builds rocks and cliffs like bricks, makes battles and 3D maps faster, and fixes a long list of things found by using the editor and the game in unusual orders. The full technical list is in the [editor changelog](../editor/changelog/0.98.9.md).

### Added

**Rocks and cliffs**

- **A Rock slot in the Build bar.** Pick a size (Boulder, Rock, Cliff, Spire, Ledge, Slab), click to lay a rock, click one to stack another on it. Rocks laid side by side and stacked melt into one craggy mass with ledges and cracks, so a row of bricks reads as a cliff.
- **Drag lays a row, Ctrl-drag fills an area.** With Vary on, each rock gets its own size, a slight turn and roughness; the ghost always shows the rock the next click lays.
- **Tops and tints.** A rock's top can wear any material or the map's own ground, so a cliff top is the grass around it. Tint colors its sides. The Demo gains Rock, Grass, Moss and Snow materials.
- **Walked on as drawn.** A party stands on the rock it sees, is stopped by its bulges and walks under a raised rock.

### Improved

- **Faster battles.** A battle's 3D room draws straight into the game's own picture instead of being copied through the CPU every frame, and once per frame shown. A Demo battle on a software renderer went from 249 to about 95 ms a frame.
- **Faster 3D maps and editor.** Lights are sent to the GPU only when they change, the reflection capture rests while nothing shiny is on screen, reflections and glass no longer make every material reload its shader, and off-screen videos stop uploading frames.
- **Ponds follow the ground.** Digging under a lake or stamping a building into it refits the water, so no see-through patches are left behind.

### Fixed

- **Undo does what you expect in every tool.** Ctrl+Z mid-drag, after switching tools, in Lighting, Media Surfaces and Event mode, and across terrain and building steps takes back the right thing and keeps other tools' work.
- **Nothing is lost by accident.** Copy and Paste with a building slot in hand no longer pastes a copy of the whole map; clearing a structure's name no longer deletes its file; Import Project refuses the project you have open; the event editor is safe from undo underneath it.
- **Escape closes the innermost dialog**, not the whole Database. Viewing a record no longer counts as an edit.
- **Events of any size own their whole footprint** in the 2D editor too.
- **In the game:** a Text Input no longer leaves a key held, the menu stays shut while a fatal fall ends the game, and memory no longer grows with every battle.
- **Faster on weak graphics.** On an Intel UHD 630 the start map now walks at about 115 FPS at 1080p, where it began at 17: the 3D is drawn at three quarters of the screen and enlarged sharp, lighting and light haze are worked out at a smaller size and filtered up, lights that hold still are baked, moving lights are gathered once a frame, shadows draw simplified outlines, and the editor draws only when something changes. New things appear as they load instead of stopping the game.
- **Clean edges on fast settings.** Models no longer pick up blocks of the ground's light around their edges, laser beams stay crisp lines, and some floors take the shadows they had been missing.
- **Ryzen laptops on Linux** get the settings made for integrated graphics.
- **MV projects open straight from RPG Maker MV** boot without visiting System 2 first, and MV plugins that read an event's empty page no longer stop the map.
- **MV games look and play as they do in MV.** Scrolling layer graphics follow the camera, floating speech bubbles fit their text, a choice answers once, and windows, menus and choice lists keep MV's padding, alignment and transparency.
- **Every party member casts a shadow** on integrated graphics, not only the leader.
- **A GPU reset no longer breaks the game.** After a driver reset, sleep or graphics crash the 3D map, battle rooms, window text and effects come back instead of a black screen or empty windows.
- **Importer:** titles in Japanese and Western European games read correctly, maps numbered 1000 and up import, and XP imports no longer risk running the editor out of memory.
- **RPG Maker 2000/2003 imports get their RTP on their own.** The import dialog looks for the RTP as soon as you pick a game; when there is none, **Download the RTP** fetches RPG Maker's official free RTP for that engine and unpacks it, without installing anything. The RTP field takes a folder, an installer or an archive (.exe, .zip, .rar, including Don Miguel's RTPE.exe), picked or dropped on it, and unpacks what needs unpacking. Older English games made with Don Miguel's RTP translation are matched to the official RTP's files, the best-fitting RTP on your computer is chosen for each game, and the dialog names any files only his RTP Addon has. The game's RM2000 font is found in the RTP folder or your system fonts. The RTP is chosen afresh for each game, and Browse › Clear removes it.
- **2000/2003 titles look like the original:** the game's picture with its own three-command menu, centred low, without MZ's drawn game title on top.
- **RPG Maker VX games import with their own systems.** Thrall comes across with its title, menus, inventory and loot, skill trees, crafting, side-view active-time battles, message window, lighting, fog, overlay maps and procedural dungeons, ported from its scripts. VX games also get their screen size and font from the game, and their own key layout.
- **RPG Maker VX Ace tactics games import with their own systems.** Pinnacle - The Five Kingdoms comes across with its grid battles (GubiD's GTBS: movement and attack ranges, facing, terrain, army units, enemy turns, damage popups), its kingdom layer (buildings, daily income, armies, story journal), crafting, its settings screen, message busts and large choice lists. Games that keep their text in language files import in the language you choose, and window frames repeat their edge ornaments as in the original.
- **More game scripts carry over.** Event scripts that wait inside a loop (a chase, a wolf moving step by step) now run, as do choice lists a script builds, scripted shops, and damage formulas that set variables.
- **Sharper XP, VX and VX Ace imports.** They open at an exact 2× (1× when that does not fit) with crisp pixels instead of a soft 1.5× stretch.
- **2000/2003 menus and battles look like the original.** The main menu, Item, Skill, Equipment and Quit screens and the battle windows take the old engines' layout, games made for RPG Maker 2003's player get its battle screen, pixel text stays sharp everywhere, battle messages name who acts, and the touch menu button is off. A game's cutscene no longer trips events it walks the hero across. Animated map tiles (pouring pipes, flowing water) move again, and water ripples at the game's own pace.
- **Walls hold on every tileset.** Tilesets made in Reactor marked the empty tile as passable, which let the party walk through walls under empty layers. The game now treats it as RPG Maker does, and new tilesets are written correctly.
- **Vehicles in 3D.** Boats ride the water and airships fly above the roofs, so the camera no longer drops under a lake.
- **Saving during an animation wait** no longer breaks the save or ends the wait on loading.
- **Change Equipment** equips the slot you pick, and enemy commands offer Entire Troop.
- **Resizing a map** moves its 3D world with the tiles, whichever anchor you pick.
- **Undo after placing a building** brings back the water and events it replaced, and a building placed in a pond stands on the water.
- **Safer projects and imports.** A damaged plugin list is never overwritten, New Project no longer copies the template's saves, game builds leave out editor files, and cancelling an import stops its helpers and asks first.
- **The editor follows a language change everywhere**, including pages already open, and the Resource Manager and many more messages are translated.
- **Walls hold water.** Pouring a pond inside a building or behind a wall keeps the water in; doorways let it through, and water higher than a wall spills over.
- **Cancel and Undo in the Database** also take back 3D model choices for actors, enemies and equipment.
