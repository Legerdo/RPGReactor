# RPG Reactor 0.99.2 (in development)

Work since 0.99.1. The short version is in the [root changelog](../../changelog/0.99.2.md). Runtime **20261024.6**.

### Changed

- Fences (runtime **20261024.6**): `Reactor3D.fenceJoins` (the four sides: a fence at its level, or a wall, pane, window or pillar it runs into; a diagonal fence when neither cell between is fence; none when joined only to walls) drives `pieceShapes` (a post and two `beam` rails toward each join) and `lineCellPiece` (the same as solids, a diagonal rail in four); `emitPiece` draws a `beam`, a box turned onto a line on the ground. Editor: a fence Line is `lineCells8`; `orientFences` turns dragged fences to the way they run; Fence leaves the panel's facing list. Straight walls: `straightWallUnder`, `straightCarved` (the wall cut either side; sill, pane and header, or a header, turned as the wall; a doorway two tiles at least), `carveInto` writes them in one go; the ghost follows. Tests `build-drawing-20261024` (and in the editor on Open Road: Fields).
- `PieceBuilderManager.wallUnder` (the wall piece a struck face or top belongs to, how high up it was struck, the face's turn, turned to look out of the building when struck from inside), `carvedPieces` (a window's foot at the aimed height, the wall under it kept, whole levels; a doorway or a door's doorway from the foot; a pane whole; a wall filling an opening) and `carveInto` (a door's doorway `door.width` cells along the wall, then the door). `dab` lays into the wall before anything else; a stroke begun on a wall paints into it piece by piece (no line or rectangle), `MapEditor3D.pieceStrokePlane` follows the walls under the pointer (`carve`), and the ghost is the carved pieces at the wall's cell. Panel hints. Test `build-drawing-20261024` (and clicked in the editor on North Haven: a window high on a wall made 8 tall, a two-wide door).
- `PieceBuilderManager.setSelectionHeight`: each storey (the selection's walls standing at one level) grows to the height, and every selected piece above its top rises by the growth (whole tiles when anything stands above, so storeys stay at whole levels); a stair or ladder in a storey that grew is flashed. Doorways and windows are `SIZED_KINDS` (runtime **20261024.5**): the lintel or header runs from `min(S, H) - 0.6` (`- 1.4`) to `H`, so the opening keeps its size. A storey is measured as built (to the next storey's walls, or the lowest floor, roof, ramp or block over its walls when its walls reach past it), so a building whose walls were made taller through the roof is put right by setting its height again. `buildingParts` reads a wall's own height for what stands on it (a gable on a taller wall stays its wall). North Haven's houses (1,588 pieces) go to 8 and back to 5 with every piece where it began. Test `build-drawing-20261024`.
- Build: `PieceBuilderManager.drawShape` (`brush`/`line`/`rect`, remembered) and `rectFill` (`auto`/`fill`/`edge`); a line is `lineCells` (4-connected) laid from the stroke's snapshot as a rectangle is, and off the axes for a wall, glass or block `diagonalWall` lays one `box` (length end to end + 1, a tile thick, the wall height, `angle` to the line) unless `diagonal` is `stepped`. `levelStep` (a quarter for floors, blocks and shapes), `raiseSelection`, Q/E on a selection, `setKind` snaps a carried level; `heights`/`setHeight` for new walls, panes, pillars. Runtime **20261024.6**: `Reactor3D.pieceHeight(kind, piece)` reads `piece.height` for `SIZED_KINDS` (0.5–40), used by `pieceShapes`, `lineCellPiece`, `hiddenFacesOf` (faces hidden only against equal heights; tops and bottoms against what stands at or ends at that level), the cutaway, lighting and Tactical; `normalizePiece` keeps quarter levels for floors and blocks (editor and runtime). BuildHotbar: Draw, Level, Position and Size cards with icons, a multi-selection Height and Level, slot key caps. Test `build-drawing-20261024`.
- Database vehicle placements on the map: `EventManager.vehicleStartOf`/`vehicleLabel`/`vehicleFootprint` (Length = the model's longest side along the start direction, width from the cached GLB extent, 1×1 for a 2D vehicle, centred), `getVehicleAt`, `canPlaceVehicle`, `moveVehicleStart` and `saveVehicleStarts` (writes `ReactorVehicles.json` on the drop and carries the vehicles into the Database window's Cancel snapshot). The 2D pointer-down takes hold of a placement and `updateDrag`/`finishDragging` move and save it; `MapEditor3D.dragEventTo` does the same; undo snapshots carry `vehicles`. The Events list gains a **Vehicles (Database)** section (`.event-list-vehicle`). Tests `vehicles-starting-position`, `map-editor-3d-events`.
- `DatabaseVehicleEditor` starting positions: each row is a grid (`1.6em | 1fr | 92px | 80px`, 30 px high) with its number always shown; the first row's button is Delete like the rest: with copies, the first copy's place and direction move into the record and its number stays retired (`copyCount`); alone, it clears to unplaced. Test `vehicles-equipment-ui`.
- Motion library on any rigged humanoid: `attachLibraryMotions` attaches every `POSES` motion as an empty clip (filled on first play), not only the six movement states; a clip rule's `library: "<id>"` plays it (`rule.clip` = `LIBRARY_CLIP_PREFIX + id`); `libraryMotionRules` skips motions without a state. Database › 3D Models: the clip card lists a Motion library group; a Motion library row adds the builder's battle `SLOTS` as library rules (`addLibraryBattleSet`). Demo Fleagus gets the set. Runtime **20261024.6**. Test `character-3d-motion-library`.
- `DatabaseActionSequenceEditor`: `updateReferences` draws each reference as a `.rr-sequence-reference` button (name, type, id, slot); `previewAs`/`previewAsChoice`/`previewingAs` set the sample action (skills, items), the held weapon and the Attack skill (weapons) or the user (actors, enemies), keeping the cast selects in step; the cast summary names the sample action; the Show Animation step's Action source is labelled "Each Action’s Own" and lists each using record's animation (`data-sequence-action-animations`). Four new strings in all 17 locales. Test `action-sequence-editing`.
- `.database-nav-section` takes the `.lit-section-header` look (3px `--color-accent` strip, `--color-bg-toolbar` band, `--color-text`); `.database-nav-item` padding 7px to 5px. The sidebar's content is 834 px against 895 px of room in a 1001 px tall Database window (1080p with a taskbar; it was 896 against 895, one pixel of scroll), measured in the editor's own window in the dark and light themes.

### Fixed

- A building made taller left its eaves behind: a roof's edge tilted down over the wall starts just under the wall's top. `setSelectionHeight` lifts with a storey's roof any shape that starts within 1.5 tiles under its top and reaches past it (its top from its size, tilt and roll); a post or a chimney from the ground stays. North Haven's eaves rise from 9 and 11 to 15 and 17 with the roof. Test `build-drawing-20261024`.
- Build panel: dragging the Height slider of a placed wall moved nothing and the panel showed 5 again: the sliders' shared handler took it for a move. It now sets the height (`applyHeight`, one undo step on release), as typing the number did. Test `build-drawing-20261024`.
- `EventManager.refreshAutoSizes` (scheduled from `renderEvents`, one run at a time; retried after 1, 2, 4… s while the 3D runtime or a model is still loading, a model request given 8 s) measures every event with a model and no size of its own by `EventEditor.measureAutoRect` and stores it with `setEventSizeAuto`; on a map with no other edits it recaptures the saved state, so the measurement alone is not an unsaved change. `EventEditor.sizeModelOf`/`measureAutoRect` are the Event Editor's own rule, shared. Test `event-marker-centre`.
- `EventEditor._sizeModel` falls back to the event's vehicle model (`RRVehicles.modelOf`) when its first page shows no picture, as `previewModelRaw` does; `refreshAutoSize` turns it by the model file's `vehicle.forward` (`EventEditor.vehicleFront`, the runtime's `forwardOf` rule), so the Demo bike measures 1×5 facing down, not 5×1. `EventManager.vehicleFootprint` measures the same way once the model is in (`RREventPreviewModels.peek`, new; the spec normalized so the cache key matches; a load that comes back empty is asked again), and until then counts the tiles its Length touches centred on its tile. Tests `event-marker-centre`, `vehicles-starting-position`.
- `Camera.lockReleased` (`reactor_3d.js`) set `scene.menuCalling` as soon as the pointer lock went with `document.hasFocus()` true, so any lock loss read as Escape: in harness runs (focus emulation on a shared display) the menu opened by itself, and a rider's Escape opened the menu since the vehicle never saw a cancel. It now waits 150 ms, drops the release on `blur` or a hidden page, skips it when the page heard Escape itself in the last 500 ms, and presses Escape (`Input.virtualClick('escape')`) so the key's own routing runs (dismount, message, menu). Map data was audited and holds nothing that calls a menu. Runtime **20261024.6**. Test `audit-runtime-3d-camera`.
- `Held.blade` turned the chest with `turnJoint` on top of its last turn. On a Mixamo body the chest it picks (the file's `Spine`) is written only by walk and run clips, and the hold is laid twice a frame (editor paint and `_holdUpdater`; spriteset and room in a battle), so the stance grew every frame until the barrel swung round and the body faced away, and it stayed after the gun was hidden. `Held.stances` records each stance; `unblade` takes it off before the next `aim`/`begin` and, from `HandGrip.update`, when no hold laid it that frame, only where nothing else posed the joint since. `MapHeld.solve` keeps its own slot. Test `held-stance` (synthetic, and Sniper Shot on the Demo's Fleagus at three frame strides).
- `ShaderWarmup.materialReady` counted a never-drawn material as last seen at time 0, so once the page clock passed 250 ms every new material read as "returning to view", compiled a second time and restarted its 5 s timeout. A material whose first compile is pending is not returning. Test `shader-warmup` (clock at 10 minutes).
- `KeyboardNavigation` remembers a dialog whose focus was refused while hidden, and `DetachedWindow` gives it the focus once its window shows, so the Database and Plugin Manager take keys on first opening. Tests `keyboard-navigation`, `detached-window-opening`.
- CI on GitHub (Node 22, committed files only, slower runners): timing budgets measure main-thread CPU time (`tests/helpers/timing.cjs`) and allow 4× under `CI`, reporting between 1× and 4×; the passage stroke times its slices with the frame's clock (`MapEdgeLabels.js`); the thirty-a-side battle runs on a clock that advances between frames; the Sunset import test skips without the project; the interaction-order, issue 54, keyboard-menu and event double-click smokes follow the editor's own windows and the awaited event window in `loadMap`.
