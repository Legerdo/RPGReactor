# RPG Reactor 0.98.9 (in development)

### Added

- **Rocks in the Build bar (runtime 20260928.3).** A Rock slot beside Shape lays rock pieces: a shape kind (`kind: "rock"`, a `size`, turned and placed like any shape, optional `rough` 0-1) that is never drawn as its box. Every rock of a map is one signed-distance field of rounded boxes, smooth-unioned where they meet (`ROCK_BLEND`), read through a slow world-space bend (`ROCK_WARP`) and roughened by world-space value noise (lumps, creases and height bands cut back into ledges), with the noise faded over each rock's top tile so tops stay walkable. `rockMesh` meshes it by surface nets on a half-tile grid a chunk at a time; a chunk owns the edges that start in it, so chunk seams are exact, and `updatePieces` relays three cells further round an edit on a map with rocks. Faces are shaded by their own facing times a vertex crack-darkening from the field, box-mapped at five tiles per image, drawn double-sided (a crease can fold a face). A rock stands, blocks and is walked on as its box (the shape rules); one on the ground reaches a tile into it. The panel's presets (Boulder, Rock, Cliff, Spire, Ledge, Slab) set its size; Rough is its setting; the first rock of a session wears the project's `Rock` material when there is one. The Demo gains `img/materials/Rock.png`. Structure plans take rocks like any shape. 8 phrases in 17 locales.
- **Rock tops, tint and the rock brush.** A rock takes `cap` (a material for faces turned up near its top and near-flat ledges lower down, with a wandering edge; `"ground"` wears the room floor image at its own scale through `MapScene.groundLoader`, else the `Grass` material) and `tint` (its sides). Rock sides use a triplanar material (`Reactor3D.triplanarMaterial`: three world-position projections blended by the field's smooth normal, which the chunk now carries as its normal attribute), so a crag's faces no longer jump between projections. The panel gains Top (None, Ground, any material), Tint (with Natural) and Vary. A placing drag lays one rock per spacing (three quarters of its width), a Ctrl rectangle fills a grid of that spacing, and with Vary on each rock scales ±25% (height −25/+30%), turns up to 25° and nudges its roughness from a seed (`PieceBuilderManager.rockVariation`), fixed ahead so the ghost is the next rock and a rectangle keeps its rocks while dragged. `pieceMaterials` lists rock tops, so the editor loads them. Chunks cache their rock meshes by the rocks that reach them, and the mesher is faster: plain-box far field, per-column rock lists, slopes from the grid, no per-sample closures (a 32×32 cliff area from 101 to about 72 ms). The Demo gains `Grass`, `Moss` and `Snow` materials. 7 phrases in 17 locales.

### Fixed

Runtime 20260928.2. Found in an audit of the 0.98.8 features; each has a Node test.

- **Movement on 3D maps.** A transfer or Set Event Location clears height, jump speed, ladder, swim and dive state (`ReactorPhysics.settle`), so a player leaving a roof no longer falls from that height on the next map, and the 2D path drops the ladder flag. A follower waiting on a ladder stores `true` instead of its leader (`$gamePlayer` holds the followers, so saving mid-climb failed with "Object too deep"). A jump key re-taken from a plugin gives back what it did before the map instead of saving "jump" as its old action. Map Properties › Jumping: On jumps where the project's Controls switch jumping off.
- **Custom interfaces.** Close All and the 16-level nesting limit compared the stack against the unwrapped `Scene_ReactorUI`; pixi_compat's wrapper never matched, so a Common Event button inside a called interface returned to the caller instead of the map (`ReactorUI.isInterfaceClass`). A Call Interface or plugin scene that cannot open (deleted id, overlay, nesting limit, a scene that throws) left the caller closing and deaf to input; `cancelClose` puts it back. Cancelling the target of an authored Use a Skill battle command returns to the command window without re-setup.
- **Reflections and mirrors.** A battle preview in the editor switched reflections off for the map view until reload; the room hands them back on dispose. The half-a-percent screen test for mirror slots 3 and 4 read the hysteresis-boosted score, so a mirror once chosen was never dropped (entries carry their `share`). Maps with no mirrors skip the frustum and matrix work, the pre-draw `resetState` and the previous map's mirror list; mirror render targets are released on map load (`Mirrors.reset`).
- **Motion editor.** Renaming a motion to a name in use deleted the other motion's rules; the name is refused. Delete after a save removed whatever rule moved into the old slot. Unnamed pose rules were duplicated on save. Ticking Stance read the first key instead of the pose at the playhead and threw on a part with no keys. A quadruped's default Walk played over the model's own walking or dashing motion. Applying a preset while the Motion editor showed that motion left it stale.
- **World builder.** Builder undo restored the whole terrain and all lights from each snapshot, wiping terrain and light edits made since; only edits that change them snapshot them now. Undo relinks a building to its plan and brings back hammered lights and screens. Select edits (material, move, delete, stair and ladder resize) unlink a stamped building from its plan, as brush edits do. Wide ladders are turned, moved, deleted and copied whole. The selection is cleared on a map switch (piece ids are per map, so Delete removed the next map's piece). Terrain undo goes through the build history: Ctrl+Z no longer also undoes a tile paint, and the history is cleared per map. The blueprint slot forgets a plan from another project, model thumbnails are cached per project, and sidecar terrain rows wrap at the grid's width.
- **A pond keeps its water where the ground under it is dug out.** A sheet's `mask` was the hollow as poured; digging an island out afterwards left it dry, a see-through square in the lake (North Haven). A terrain stroke or Clear now refits every sheet to the ground at its own level and within its box (`RRMapElevation.refitWater`): water spreads from the cells it covers to every lower neighbour and leaves ground that rose above it, and a sheet left with no cells goes. A terrain undo step holds the ground and the water together.
- **Terrain and water out of order.** Terrain undo and builder undo put back only the corners their step moved (`RRMapElevation.restoreTerrainChanges`; a terrain step keeps just those corners), so undoing a stroke no longer un-levels a building stamped since, and undoing a stamp keeps hills raised since. A stamp or group move refits the ponds on its pad. Rocks and pieces are laid again when the ground under them moves (they stayed buried until a rebuild), and a stroke under a pond relays its depth shading. Ctrl+Z mid-stroke, or another tool taken up mid-stroke, closes the stroke first. Undoing a liquid's look updates the Look panel (a slider then wrote the undone look back), and picking another sheet before a colour commits keeps it as its own step. Pour's preview goes with the tool, the Terrain panel's status follows Ctrl+Z, and Lava shows as chosen when read back. Water can stand as high as ground goes (160, was 60: a pond on a tall plateau sat under the ground).
- **Rocks are stood on and block where they are drawn (runtime 20260928.9).** A rock stood and blocked as its box while its drawn surface sat up to a tile and a half off it: a party floated half a tile over a low boulder, met invisible walls and walked through bulges. `Reactor3D.rockColumns` measures each cell's rock at its middle (the top from above, halved in; the mass's foot, so an arch is walked under) and the piece index holds those columns instead of the box footprint; measured per chunk and kept by the rocks that reach it (240 rocks: about 100 ms once, 8 ms after an edit). The world bend is also taken relative to each rock's middle (`rockFrame().warp`), so the bend shapes the outline without moving the rock up to a tile and a half from where it was laid (and from its ghost). Selection and plans still use the box.

### Performance

Runtime 20260928.4. Measured on the headless software-GL display (a stand-in for a weak GPU); frozen frames compared pixel for pixel where the change should not show.

- **Battles draw their room once per frame shown.** `P.updateRoom` rendered the three.js room, read it back into a full-screen Bitmap and uploaded it on every update, so a tick catching up did it several times for one frame. The update now marks the room and `P.flushRoom` runs from `Graphics._reactorBeforeRender` (hooks the game loop runs once, just before the draw); without a game loop it draws at once. The room's renderer drops MSAA, as the map's passes did. A Demo battle: 249 to 103 ms a frame.
- **Passes no longer make three look every program up again (runtime 20260928.7).** Two causes, both measured in the editor's 3D view. The mirror pictures and the reflection cube rendered linear while the main passes render sRGB, and output colour space is part of a program's key, so every change of pass ran `getParameters`/`getProgram` for every material; they are now written as the main passes are (`Reactor3D.sharePassOutput`: sRGB, RGBA8, the XR output flag) and every reader decodes them (`SRGB_DECODE_GLSL`: mirror slots, shine and water; the studio cube, which the GPU decodes, is read as it is). And three draws a see-through two-sided material twice, marking it changed between the draws: one glass piece material changed version 6,998 times in 30 frames. Glass pieces now draw once (`forceSinglePass`). Each material holds one program instead of two, and the lookups are gone from the orbit profile. Reflections store 8-bit sRGB rather than 8-bit linear (finer in the shadows; blurred mips average in sRGB), and glass blends its two faces in one draw: frames away from water differ by under 1 of 255 on average.
- **Light arrays are sent once per program, not on every material bind (runtime 20260928.6).** three.js skips an unchanged plain uniform but sends every uniform array each time a material is bound, so each lit material re-sent all the lights (three arrays of `SHADER_LIGHTS` vec4s and the shadow tables). `Reactor3D.cacheUniformArrays(renderer)`, run at the start of every colour draw (`renderScene`, the battle room), wraps each program's number-array uniforms to compare with the values last sent to that program and send only a change; sampler arrays are left alone. `Reactor3D.uniformArrayCache = false` sends everything again. North Haven in the game: 405 to 82 array uploads a frame. The editor's 3D view while orbiting: `uniform4fv` had been 2.55 of 5.19 s of main-thread time and is gone from the profile (the main thread idles 78% of a frame instead of 15%). Checked in the game by reading every array element back from GL after each upload, sent or held, over 8 s of walking: 9,200 uploads across 45 programs, no difference.
- **Battle rooms draw on the game's own WebGL context (runtime 20260928.5).** When the map's viewport shares PIXI's context (the default), `BattleRoomView` renders with its renderer into a render target of its own (`shareTarget`: `viewport.createTarget`, the GL texture adopted by a PIXI source, flipped) and the battle shows that texture on a plain PIXI sprite: no readback through a 2D canvas, no upload. Effects run on the viewport's effect context (`GpuEffects.forViewport`), every three.js pass hands PIXI its state back (`releaseContext`, `_resetPixi`), and the target is released two frames after the room. `Reactor3D.battleRoomShared = false`, `?r3dcopy`, or a PIXI without WebGL 2 keep the canvas copy. On the software-GL harness the texture upload had been 87% of the main thread in a battle (7.96 of 9.10 s); it is gone, and the main thread now idles 86% of a battle frame. Checked in the game: two battles with a room effect and back to North Haven each time, and a battle started before any map made the viewport; no GL or console errors, pictures matching the copy path.
- **The reflection capture waits while nothing shiny is on screen.** Shiny materials note the frame their own draw ran (`Reflections.bind`); once the cube is whole, a frame after one that showed nothing shiny takes no face and skips the early `resetState` (`Reflections.idle`). North Haven's 3D update: about 1.84 to 1.36 ms a frame.
- **Off-screen videos stop uploading frames.** `updatePixiPosition` judges each media surface against the screen (96 px margin) and turns its video source's uploads off while it is off screen; playback goes on and the frame uploads the moment it returns. The flat Demo map: 730 to 472 uploads per 4 s, 33 to 41 fps.
- **Smaller per-frame waste:** particles look up each texture once a frame instead of per particle (2000 particles: 2.36 to 1.65 ms); character sorting keeps its helpers and array (`reactorSortedZ`); the light march's solid-grid cache compares numbers instead of building a key (`lightSolidGrid`, 20 to 7 ns a sample); model collision keys are kept per spec (`collisionKeysFor`); `syncLights` and `mirrorCandidates` reuse their vectors, matrix and bounds.

Runtime 20260930.2. Measured on an Intel UHD 630 (i5-10500H, the laptop's integrated GPU forced on) at 1920×1080 with `editor/tests/perf/nw-frame-breakdown.cjs`: per-pass and per-draw GPU timer queries, spike attribution (a CPU profile lined up with each long frame, plus the GL traffic the page handed the GPU process in it), and a frozen A/B mode that draws the same frame under a timer and compares every variant's pixels with the baseline's own run-to-run noise.

- **One shadow lookup instead of two.** `rrShadowAt` worked out the cube face, projection and depth once per atlas through a helper taking the sampler, and both copies sat inlined in the light loop; their presence alone made every lit pixel slower, shadowed or not. The face and projection are computed once and the static and dynamic atlases sampled from them. Same result; a frozen Demo frame 29.8 to 23.9 ms.
- **Lighting after the discards.** The light term multiplied the colour where three first sets it, so a fragment the alpha test, the zero-alpha skip, dissolve or the cutaway was about to throw away still ran the whole light loop. It now multiplies after `alphahash_fragment` (`Reactor3D.lateLighting`, `LIT_MARK`); a flash colour blends over the lit colour after it, with the vertex colour, exactly as before. A Glow (self-lit) never enters the loop.
- **Blank tiles leave the draw.** A tile whose clamped sample rectangle holds no pixel above alpha 0 (every texel bilinear filtering can reach) is dropped from its group's index at build (`Reactor3D.InvisibleTiles`, game only; the editor raycasts them). The Demo's start map had 2,107 blank A5 floor tiles drawn twice, each lit and then discarded.
- **Characters skinned only into what can see them.** `Reactor3D.InstanceCull` culls a skinned instance by a world sphere from where it stands and its span (1.5 spans and a tile), never by the skin's rest bounds, per shadow face and for the view. A character was skinned into all six faces of every shadow row; 6.1 to 1.5 draws a frame. A ragdoll is never culled.
- **Static shadow rows ignore distance-level swaps.** They always draw each model's coarsest level, but any swap in the view re-rendered every static row, about once a frame while walking (`Shadows.lodSwapsDirtyStatic`, off).
- **Skinned characters get distance levels.** `GeometryDetail` simplifies skinned meshes too (index only; skin weights guide the simplifier so no edge collapses across a joint), measured from the placed instance, not the skin's node.
- **Media surfaces share their video.** Muted surfaces playing one file with the same loop and speed use one `<video>` and, on a 3D map, one texture uploaded once a frame (`MediaSurfaceManager.joinVideo`); the last to leave stops it, one asked to start elsewhere keeps its own. The start map's ten video screens went from ten decoders to two.
- **The Effekseer overlay rests.** It was cleared every frame, a new full-screen picture for the compositor to blend over the game; it is cleared only after something drew there and hidden while empty (`Graphics.showEffekseerOverlay`).
- **Effect targets grow instead of reallocating.** An anchored effect's target followed its box's size every frame, freeing and reallocating its texture, framebuffer and multisample buffer; it grows in steps of 64 and the quad samples the drawn corner (`uvScale`).
- **Outside the light grid, only the lights that reach there.** A fragment outside the grid scanned every light; it loops over the lights whose reach leaves the grid (`LightGrid.spill`, and suns). The grid is 16 cells high (64 units) so a room's ceiling is inside it.
- **Release builds skip three's synchronous shader checks** (a wait on the GPU per new program); playtest and the editor keep them (`Reactor3D.setShaderErrorChecks`).
- **The editor draws only when something changes.** The 2D map rendered every frame forever, behind the Database too; `MapRenderScheduler` (TilemapManager.js) takes PIXI's render off the ticker and draws on input, on PIXI's own queued changes, on uploads, while something animates, and once a second as a net; nothing while the Database covers it. The 3D view renders on demand with a short tail and runs continuously only while something animates; the Lighting loop no longer resolves every light each frame; Action Sequence, event-page, model-picker, model-preview and 3D-database previews draw nothing when still. Tile layers leave PIXI's hit test (a map-sized hit surface stands in), wheel zoom and pans are applied once a frame with the panel size cached, the translation observer handles only added elements and does not run in English, list search waits 150 ms, the profiler wrapper is off unless `rr.perfProfiler` is set, and the event hover outline is rebuilt only on a new tile. `map-render-on-demand.test.cjs`.
- **Shadow faces draw simplified casters (runtime 20260930.3).** A shadow face holds a silhouette 256 or 512 texels across; each caster now takes the coarsest simplified level whose error, seen from the light at that face's resolution, is under half a texel (`GeometryDetail.forShadow`, `SHADOW_TEXEL_ERROR`), skinned characters measured from their placed instance. The start map's static rows went from 492k to 163k triangles a render and its moving-caster rows from 149k to 11k; the owner saw no difference.
- **Still lights come from a baked light volume.** `Reactor3D.LightVolume`: a light whose settings and static shadow row have held for 30 frames is baked on the GPU (a few layers a frame, into a back buffer that swaps in whole) into a half-tile RGBA16F 3D texture over the map, and each lit pixel reads it once, stepping a little off its face toward the eye, instead of working that light out again; the light leaves the per-pixel loop (`rrLightCached`). A flickering light is baked unlit into one of four channels of a second volume and its colour times this frame's flicker applied per pixel. Moving and beam lights, and lights holding a moving-caster shadow row (the party's shadows), stay live and sharp (pulsing and moving lights: see below); shadows baked into the volume are half a tile soft, a trade the owner accepted. A cached light that changes sets the volume aside until the new one is baked; a battle room or preview packing its own lights switches it off. A model's effect light counts too (runtime 20260930.4): where it stands and aims is in its key, so a glow on a model that holds still is baked and one on a swinging arm stays live. Start map: 12 of 23 lights baked (8 still, 4 flickering), 6.4 ms of a frozen frame (41.9 to 35.5 ms); on for 395 of 407 frames of play, a re-bake only when a static shadow row redrew. `light-volume.test.cjs`.
- **Pulsing lights join the volume (runtime 20260930.5).** A pulse only breathes its radius on a fixed cycle, so the volume keeps each pulsing point light's distance (four channels of a third volume, `rrVolumePulse`) and every pixel applies this frame's radius and colour with the loop's own falloff; the light leaves the per-pixel loop. Point lights without a shadow row only; `nativeLights` passes the pre-pulse `baseRadius` so the key holds still. Start map: 15 of 23 lights baked (8 still, 4 flickering, 3 pulsing), 8.3 ms of a frozen frame.
- **Moving lights are gathered once a frame, not per pixel (runtimes 20260930.5–.6).** Every point or spot light the volume does not hold, with no shadow row and a reach of at least four tiles (the sweeping searchlights, the screen glows on swinging arms), is baked each frame in one draw into an atlas of stacked slices (`LightVolume.frameDynamic`, `rrDynAtlas`, read with a two-slice blend) and leaves the per-pixel loop (`rrLightDynamic`). Its grid is a tile (`DYNAMIC_VOXEL`; the falloff of a light without a shadow is smooth over its whole reach), an eighth of the half-tile bake's work; it covers the box of the lit meshes the camera sees, on the volume's own grid; and it is not baked again while none of its lights moved, so frames drawn faster than the game's logic reuse it. Lights with a shadow row stay per pixel, their shadows sharp (`dynamicShadowRows` bakes still rows too). On the start map it evens out on average and saves 3.5 ms in the view with the most swinging lights. Kill switch `LightVolume.dynamicEnabled`.
- **Things appear as they load instead of stopping the frame (runtime 20260930.5).** `Reactor3D.ShaderWarmup`: a mesh whose material has never been compiled is left out of the draw while its program compiles on the driver's threads (`KHR_parallel_shader_compile`, `compileAsync` with the pass bound, six a frame, drawn anyway after 5 s), then shows; a material recompiled after it was first drawn is never held. Its pictures are decoded off the main thread (`Reactor3D.ImageWorker`: fetch and `createImageBitmap` in a worker, the bitmap transferred back already flipped) and uploaded under a 16 MB a frame budget before it shows. The covered-floor opacity check moved to the worker too. The light volume's bake waits for its own program the same way, and uniform caches skip programs still compiling. Fresh profile, 15 s from New Game on the start map: 298 → 453 frames, worst frame 2.4 s → 0.62 s (what remains is Effekseer's one-time WebAssembly start and single large texture uploads). `shader-warmup.test.cjs`.
- **Room walls share one texture.** Each wall piece cloned its texture with its own source, so one picture was uploaded once per wall; they share the source now. Skinned models preset their bounding box with their sphere, so the first frame does not walk every vertex.
- **Model sync and animation reuse their work (runtime 20260930.3).** `Reactor3D.StillModels`: a model whose placement inputs and root transform are exactly what its last placement left is not placed again, and its root matrix is frozen while it holds (the player, climbers and ragdolls always placed). `Reactor3D.AnimationMatches`: which rules reach which parts is a table per binding, rebuilt when the rules, a rule, its part, the meshes or a part chain change, instead of matched and sorted every frame; `Reactor3D.AnimationWrites` skips writing a quaternion that is already what this pass wrote. Keyed poses sample into reused arrays (`_sampleModelKeysInto`), and the pose, transform and billboard paths stop allocating. 3D media surfaces are placed once a frame instead of twice. Same digest of every node's transforms and colours over 900 frames with the caches on and off; a Map001-like scene's model pass 138 to 107 µs a frame, the player's animation 51 to 24 µs. `model-frame-caches.test.cjs`, `media-surface-frame-caches.test.cjs`.
- **An empty pass is not composited.** A pass that drew nothing (the upper pass on a map with models in the world) no longer has its full-screen transparent sprite blended over the frame (`EmptyPass.hideEmpty`); 0.4 ms on the start map, identical pixels.
- **Smaller:** a media surface's descriptor is checked once a frame, not twice per tick.
- **The light is worked out at a quarter size (runtime 20260930.6).** `Reactor3D.HalfLight`: a map pass draws every surface whose colour is its picture times the light unlit first (an unlit twin program per material, sharing everything else by inheritance), into a target sharing the pass's depth; then the light, `rrLight` at each texel's world point rebuilt from that depth, at a quarter of the size each way, with the face a point lies on taken from the full-size depth around it; then one pass writes the pass as the unlit colour back in linear, times the light filtered up, encoded again; then everything else lit as before (reflective finishes, flashes, glows, glass, the haze). The light has no texture in it, so the lower grid shows only at the rim of a model against a much brighter or darker ground; and it is worked out once a pixel, not once per surface drawn over it (the start map's tree lies six leaves deep). Maps with water draw as before. Start map, frozen, three places on the walk: 24.8 ms before this round, 11.0 ms after, with every change in this round. `half-size-light.test.cjs`.
- **The lights' haze at a quarter size (runtime 20260930.6).** `Reactor3D.HalfHaze`: the cones and balls of haze (a smooth gradient over much of the screen, added once per face) are drawn into a quarter-size target, each fragment testing its distance from the eye against the pass's (the light pass keeps it in its alpha; otherwise the depth is shrunk once, the farthest of each block, so haze runs a little over an edge rather than stopping short of it), and added onto the pass in one draw. The bright cores and a laser's dot stay sharp. 3.8 ms of eleven cones at 1080p became about 0.8.
- **Distance levels at a pixel of error.** `GeometryDetail.pixelError` 0.35 → 1: beside it only single texels on the characters moved (a mean difference of 0.09 of a level); 0.85 ms of a frame.
- **The game canvas is opaque.** It was created with an alpha channel for a 3D canvas that once sat underneath it; the 3D draws into the scene now, and the browser was blending the whole canvas over the page every frame. At 144 Hz on the UHD 630 that pushed most frames past the refresh: walking the start map 91 → 101 fps, the median frame 13.7 → 7 ms.
- **The 3D is drawn at three quarters and enlarged sharp (runtime 20260930.9).** `Reactor3D.renderScale` is 0.75 by default: the pass is drawn into a smaller target and enlarged into a full-size one by `Reactor3D.SharpUpscale` instead of PIXI enlarging it pixel for pixel. "cas" (the default, at sharpness 0.5: bilinear, sharpened within its four neighbours' range) or "crisp" (each source pixel flat, its edge blended over half a screen pixel at sharpness 1: hard and slightly stepped, too sharp on a second screen). ### Development

- **The frame harness times frozen frames back to back.** `nw-frame-breakdown.cjs --mode=frozen` slept between timed draws, and an integrated GPU dropped its clock in the gaps: the same frame measured 12.9 to 20.4 ms. It now draws eight frames first and times the rest back to back (±0.1 ms). `--positions=N --gap=S` freezes N places along a `--walk`; `--images` saves each variant's frame; it waits for shader warmup, the light volume and a resting camera before freezing.
- **CI.** The Web persistence smoke is intermittent on GitHub, not broken. On failure it now prints the page state and browser console, and on Actions repeats them as an error annotation, which the public API returns without signing in (`reportSmokeFailure` in `webdriver-client.cjs`). `actions/checkout` and `actions/setup-node` move to v5 (Node 24; v4's Node 20 is deprecated on runners).
- **`reactor_core.js` and `reactor_objects.js` split by class (runtime 20260929.1).** The core (10,700 lines) is now `reactor_core.js` (JS extensions, Utils, Graphics, profiler, events, Point, Rectangle, Bitmap), `reactor_core_display.js` (Sprite to Stage) and `reactor_core_io.js` (WebAudio, Video, Input, TouchInput, JsonEx). The objects (12,800 lines) are `reactor_objects.js` (Game_Temp to Game_Item), `_actions`, `_battlers`, `_map`, `_interpreter` and `_legacy` (the 2000/2003 command equivalents and `[fN]` sheets). Every part is an exact slice of the old file, cut at a section boundary; `reactor_main.js` loads them back to back and the three deploy checks require them. Globals, class names and load order are unchanged, so plugins and imports see the same engine. One hazard, found by loading the parts in a VM: `Window.defaultMargin` was set in the Bitmap section, before `function Window()`, which only worked because declarations hoist within one file (split, a browser would set it on the DOM's `Window` and lose it); it moved into the Window section. `runtime-core-split.test.cjs` loads the parts in order with only PIXI stubbed, so a part reaching a later part's class while loading fails; tests read the source through `tests/helpers/runtime-source.cjs` (`runtimeSource()`, the parts in load order); `SequencePreview` reads WebAudio from `reactor_core_io.js`, or `reactor_core.js` in an unrefreshed project. The runtime root cap in `reactor-3d-foundation.test.cjs` is 35.
- **Headless harness displays.** On the headless Wayland compositor (`WAYLAND_DISPLAY=rroracle`) Chromium's GPU process has no hardware GL and runs on SwiftShader, while video still decodes on the real GPU; the decoded frames cannot be imported (`Could not find SharedImageBackingFactory ... MailboxVideoFrameConverter`) and the GPU process crashes, three times a Demo session, taking every WebGL context with it. On the X11 display (`XDG_SESSION_TYPE=x11 DISPLAY=:57`) the game renders on the real GPU and nothing crashes. Harnesses must set one of the two explicitly: NW.js follows the desktop session's `XDG_SESSION_TYPE=wayland` and fails to start otherwise.
- **Harness windows are silent and named.** A sound mixer remembers a mute per program name, and NW.js says "Chromium": a harness window muted in the mixer muted every Chromium browser afterwards (WirePlumber's `Output/Audio:application.name:Chromium`). `webdriver-client.cjs` starts sessions with `--mute-audio` (`RR_NW_AUDIO=1` keeps sound) and names them `RPG Reactor harness` (`PULSE_PROP_OVERRIDE`); `RPGReactor.sh` names the editor and its playtests `RPG Reactor`.
- **Frozen-mode comparisons and shader warmup.** `nw-frame-breakdown.cjs --mode=frozen` stops the clock; a variant whose programs were never compiled (the full-size path, with `HalfLight` on all session) can be held by the warmup and draw wrongly or not at all. Judge looks with the clock running and game updates stopped, each variant drawn for a second before the capture.
- **Height brush leftovers removed.** The comments and the five `toolbar.height*` / `toolbar.title.heightBrush` strings (18 locales) of the removed Map Properties height brush; stale code comments on `PIECE_MAX_LEVEL` (48 storeys) and rock collision (rocks stand where drawn) corrected.
